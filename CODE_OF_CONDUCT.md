# Code of Conduct

Easie follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful, whatever someone's background or experience.
- Give and take feedback kindly. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or personal attacks.
- Don't share anyone's private information without their permission.

This applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer, [@Mrabbi3](https://github.com/Mrabbi3), privately
(for example through GitHub's [private reporting](https://github.com/Mrabbi3/Easie/security/advisories/new)
form, noting that it is a conduct report). Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that don't follow this code, and
may warn, or temporarily or permanently ban, anyone whose behaviour is harmful, as the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines describe.
